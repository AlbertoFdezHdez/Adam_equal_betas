# Verification record

This record distinguishes execution from inspection. It does not claim that the costly training grids were rerun during packaging.

## Executed during the audit

1. **Lightweight numerical tests:** `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` completed 5/5 tests. The tests cover bias-corrected EMA behavior, inclusive sign windows and zero filtering, exact decomposition identity and budget normalization, EMA/first/second differences, and non-finite selection behavior.
2. **Controlled figures:** `analysis/generate_controlled_figures.py` completed and produced both PDFs plus the protocol summary JSON.
3. **Digits smoke pipeline:** a 12-step full-batch trajectory with all 3466 coordinates was trained and decomposed for windows 2 and 6. Both rows had exact `max_identity_error = 0.0` in float64.
4. **Full archived Digits trajectory:** one original `(1500,3466)` float32 trajectory for `(beta1,beta2)=(0.9,0.999)` was analyzed over all coordinates. Its 1000-step and 6-step outputs matched the archived analysis. For `w=1000`, the executed values were `N=995852`, `S=41.949278%`, `L_disc=44.841481%`, `E=13.209241%`, `kappa=1.663556`, `F2=63.5175%`, sign agreement `99.62735%`, and identity error `0.0`. This is one seed, not the three-seed aggregate shown in the manuscript.
5. **Archived six-task analysis:** the final analyzer was executed privately on the five available historical pickle result sets and the complete 75-file ResNet JSON grid. Comparison with the definitive manuscript values reported 0 mismatches and maximum absolute error 0 for all 70 Table-2 rows and all 30 Table-4 rows. No source or generated result file is bundled.
6. **Second-difference convention:** both the interior `T-2` convention and the optional one-sided-boundary convention were run on all six archived datasets. They produced identical published Table-4 selection rates.
7. **Static/package checks:** all Python sources compiled; all three JSON configurations parsed; all three shell launchers passed `bash -n`; PyTorch, TorchVision, Transformers, NumPy, Matplotlib, scikit-learn, and the six experiment command-line entry points imported successfully in the audit environment.

## Verified by inspection

- The literal decomposition recurrence, zero initialization, bias correction `1-beta**(k+1)`, epsilon placement, dtype conversion, pooled budgets, `F2`, cancellation diagnostic, sign agreement, and window indexing were traced from source to the definitive manuscript protocol.
- Every manuscript figure/table has an explicit production path in `MANUSCRIPT_CODE_MAP.md`.
- All six beta grids, seed sets, model/dataset preprocessing paths, optimizer epsilon values, batch sizes, sequence lengths, and gradient-accumulation behavior were read from the entry points and recorded in `configs/experiment_protocols.json`.
- PDF metadata and extracted text were inspected for author/institution identifiers.

## Not executed during the audit

- The 27 complete 1500-step Digits training runs were not repeated. The archived three-seed aggregate CSVs were checked privately but are not included in this code-only package; one complete trajectory was executed through the final analyzer.
- None of the six costly model--dataset training grids was rerun.
- No large dataset, pretrained model, or external nanoGPT repository was downloaded.
- The missing `tiktoken` and `kaggle` optional packages were not installed in the audit host; they are declared in `requirements-training.txt`, and their dependent paths were checked statically rather than executed.

## Known fidelity limitations

- Historical files labelled “EfficientNet-B0” were generated by EfficientNet-V2-S; the manuscript now uses the correct model name.
- Its final 200-class classifier was constructed after sinusoidal initialization and therefore used the default PyTorch initialization.
- The ViT/CIFAR-100 archived runs used a 1000-output head.
- The empirical `updates_norm` is restricted to directly owned linear/convolutional parameters and includes weight-decay effects.
- The exact historical nanoGPT revision and complete frozen Python environment are unavailable.
- The ResNet entry point does not itself disable TF32.
- NanoGPT scheduler cadence is not a conventional per-optimizer-step 20% warm-up.

These limitations are disclosed rather than normalized away in code.
